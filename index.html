SolarGridPulse is built with a local-first architecture. Your solar, battery, and property telemetry stays on your own network and device—Outback Telemetry does not run cloud telemetry servers, display advertising, or sell personal data.

1. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

  • Inverter & Local Telemetry: When you connect to a Selectronic SP-PRO, Select.live, Fronius inverter, or SolarGridPulse Web Server on your local Wi-Fi/LAN (or personal VPN), telemetry is fetched directly over your network and displayed on your device. It is never transmitted to Outback Telemetry.
  • App Preferences & Custom Assets: Your connection settings, Time-of-Use (TOU) tariff schedules, load profiles, 3D node positions, and any custom theme or aerial property photos you choose to import are stored locally on your device.
  • Location Coordinates (Site Setup): If you enter a site latitude and longitude (or use device location during setup), those coordinates are stored locally to calculate sunrise/sunset times and optional solar forecasts.

2. Optional Third-Party Services

Certain optional features contact third-party services directly from your device only when enabled or configured by you:

  • Open-Meteo Weather & Solar Forecast: Sends your configured site latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo to retrieve local weather, irradiance, and sunrise/sunset forecasts. No personal identifiers are attached.
  • TankMate Water Tank Monitoring: If you enter your TankMate UID and API key, the app queries api.tankmate.app directly from your device to display rainwater tank levels.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases and upgrades are processed securely by Google Play. Outback Telemetry does not collect or store your payment card details.

3. Optional Crash Reporting

If you enable optional crash reporting in Settings, the app sends diagnostic crash logs (stack trace, app version, and Android device/OS version) solely to help Outback Telemetry diagnose and fix bugs. You can toggle this off at any time in Settings.

4. Permissions Used

  • Local Network / Wi-Fi State: Used to discover and communicate with your inverter or local SolarGridPulse server on your private LAN.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used to alert you locally about blackouts, low battery state-of-charge, or sensor thresholds.
  • Photos / Files (Optional): Used only when you explicitly import a custom theme archive, aerial property photo, or CSV history file.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your configuration, history, and imported themes are stored locally on your device, you can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling SolarGridPulse. If you have submitted an optional crash report or support email and would like it deleted, contact muzza22au+solargridpulsebugs@gmail.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as new features are added. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
